# Related projects

Cancer Clicker NG is a stylized learning game, not a clinical model or source of medical advice.
The resources below help visitors explore the idle-game interaction grammar and the cancer-biology
concepts that the game adapts into explicit, limited abstractions.

## Confirmed related projects

### Cookie Clicker

- Relationship: Prior art or inspiration.
- Link: [Cookie Clicker](https://orteil.dashnet.org/cookieclicker/)
- Why visitors may care: It demonstrates the large direct-action object, persistent count and rate,
  and always-visible producer-store loop that Cancer Clicker NG adapts for a cell-division game.
- Evidence: The game's official page presents a click-driven idle loop with production per second,
  producer buildings, quantities, prices, and a Store; this repository's design contract names it
  as the source of its center-progression and right-store spatial grammar.

## Possible related projects

### Hallmarks of Cancer: New Dimensions

- Relationship: Domain standard, guide, dataset, or other visitor resource.
- Link: [Hallmarks of Cancer: New Dimensions](https://aacrjournals.org/cancerdiscovery/article/12/1/31/675608/Hallmarks-of-Cancer-New-Dimensions)
- Why visitors may care: It gives a research-level conceptual framework for the cancer capabilities,
  enabling characteristics, and proposed dimensions that this game turns into bounded player choices.
- Evidence: The American Association for Cancer Research's 2022 review describes the core
  capabilities and discusses plasticity, epigenetic reprogramming, microbiomes, and senescent cells.
- Confidence: likely

### Cancer Research UK's cancer-growth guide

- Relationship: Domain standard, guide, dataset, or other visitor resource.
- Link: [How cancers grow](https://www.cancerresearchuk.org/about-cancer/what-is-cancer/how-cancer-start-grow-spread/how-cancers-grow)
- Why visitors may care: It offers accessible background for the game's deliberately simplified
  tumor-growth, hypoxia, angiogenesis, invasion, and spread motifs.
- Evidence: Cancer Research UK explains how growing tumors become distant from oxygen and nutrients,
  induce blood-vessel growth, and can invade surrounding tissue.
- Confidence: likely

## Evidence notes

Cookie Clicker is confirmed prior art because the repository's own visual-design decision explicitly
names it as an interaction and layout reference. The two cancer resources are likely related because
their official descriptions support the same teaching workflow, while this repository's
[MORPHOLOGY_REFERENCE.md](MORPHOLOGY_REFERENCE.md) records the game's limits: it presents stylized
state cues rather than diagnosis, prognosis, or a measurement instrument.
